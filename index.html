Projects

TB Burden and Risk Toolkit

An open-source Streamlit app with a symptom and risk-factor screen, burden and care-cascade estimates (case detection, case fatality, DALYs), airborne transmission risk with the Wells-Riley equation, and population attributable fractions. MIT license, citable DOI.

Educational use only. Not a diagnostic tool and not clinically validated.
